Episode 8: Never packed a boot

Air date:
09/11/2023
Title coined by:
Lucy Beaumont

Episode synopsis

In this episode, the contestants must bring in the best drum-roll-worthy item, find the secret task, complete the fortune trail, convince a child that they are sleeping, and mime proverbs to their teams.

Title context

Lucy Beaumont mentions that she’s also “never packed a boot”, after explaining to Greg during the ‘Complete the fortune trail’ task that the reason she is so bad at coin-flipping is because there has always been a man around to do it.

Alternate Titles

  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'En ole koskaan pakannut autoa', which translates as 'I've never packed a car'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Sweden's SVT Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Aldrig packat en sko', which translates as 'Never packed a shoe'.
