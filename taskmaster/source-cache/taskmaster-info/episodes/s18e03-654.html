Episode 3: The gangsters of the sea

Air date:
26/09/2024
Title coined by:
Greg Davies

Episode synopsis

In this episode, the contestants must: bring in the object with the most soul; put a rocket in their pocket; recreate a famous 2D piece of art in 3D; win a pub quiz by cheating; and then rustle Russell, fill Phil, mark Mark, rob Rob, and pat Pat.

Title context

Greg Davies notes that Babatunde Aléshé had “labelled the orcas the gangsters of the sea” after Babatunde decided to refer to an orca as a “killer whale” because “that sounds gangster”, during his attempt at the ‘Put a rocket in your pocket’ task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Merten gangsterit', which translates as 'The gangsters of the sea'.
  • On Norway's Discovery+ Norge streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Havets gangstere', which translates as 'The gangsters of the sea'. [Translator credit: Jan Harald]
