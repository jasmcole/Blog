Episode 7: You’ve got no chutzpah

Air date:
29/04/2021
Title coined by:
Mike Wozniak

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in best thing to hang on a wall that wouldn't normally hang on a wall, complete the most tasks, make the best Russian nesting doll meal, get out of the front gate without setting of an alarm, and make their rubber gloves and wellies as heavy as possible.

Title context

'You’ve got no chutzpah' was one of the things Mike Wozniak said in an effort to undermine a vole, during the 'Complete the most tasks' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Du savner frækhed', which translates as 'You lack audacity'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Tuskaa ja maatuskaa', which translates as 'Pain and matryoshka dolls'. Unclear what the 'pain' part is referencing, but the matroyshka dolls are clearly a reference to the 'Make the best babushka meal' task. The title is punny in Finnish, since both words end in 'tuskaa'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Sweden's SVT Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Den stora babushka-banketten', which translates as 'The great babushka banquet'. This is a reference to the 'Make the best babushka meal' task. [Translator credit: DANI]
