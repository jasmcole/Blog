Episode 5: Stay humble

Air date:
05/06/2019
Title coined by:
Joe Thomas

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the best face, move sand from one bucket to another, make a realistic injury using food, play long-distance catch with a beach ball, herd ping pong balls into a pen, and recreate a scene from memory.

Title context

'Stay humble' was Joe Thomas' advice for people handling birthday candles, during the 'Make a realistic injury using food' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Forbliv ydmyg', which translates as 'Stay humble'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Pysykää nöyränä', which translates as 'Stay humble'. [Translator credit: zador]
