Episode 7: The integrity of the product

Air date:
04/11/2021
Title coined by:
Victoria Coren Mitchell

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the most ridiculous thin thing, put up a shelf for the Taskmaster's snooker balls, write and perform a 30-second jingle, pass everything through a pipe and the pipe through everything, and direct drawings using limited keywords.

Title context

'The integrity of the product' was the reason Victoria Coren Mitchell gave when asked why she was putting the snakes back into the can, in the 'Write and perform a 30-second jingle' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Produktets integritet', which translates as 'The integrity of the product'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Tuotteen eheys', which translates as 'Integrity of the product'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Sweden's SVT Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Snooker, rör och snokar i rör', which translates as 'Snooker, pipes, and snakes in pipes'. The title is referencing the snooker balls in the 'Put up a shelf for the Taskmaster's snooker balls' task, the pipe in the 'Pass everything through the pipe and the pipe through everything' task, and the small canned snakes in the 'Write and perform a 30-second jingle' task. The word 'snok' is normally used to refer to a small type of snake typically found in bushes (the word used for snakes more generally is 'orm'). [Translator credit: DANI]
  • On Norway's Discovery+ Norge streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Produktets integritet', which translates as 'The integrity of the product'. Note: this Norwegian translation of the title only appears in the subtitles for the episode, whereas the episode title listed on the platform is the original English title. [Translator credit: Jan Harald]
