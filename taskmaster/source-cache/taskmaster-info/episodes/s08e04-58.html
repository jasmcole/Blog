Episode 4: The barrel dad

Air date:
29/05/2019
Title coined by:
Greg Davies

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the best present for a doctor, get a toilet roll through a toilet seat, entertain a toddler, get the most weight into a hammock, and throw a frying pan into a hoop. In an exciting tie-breaker, three contestants compete to see who can most quicly whip a bottle off of a barrel.

Title context

'The barrel dad' was how Greg Davies described Paul Sinha's role during the 'Get the most weight into a hammock' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Tøndefaderen', which translates as 'The barrel dad'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Tynnyri-isä', which translates as 'Barrel dad'. [Translator credit: zador]
