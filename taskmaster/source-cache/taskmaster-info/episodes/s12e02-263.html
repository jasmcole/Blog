Episode 2: Oatmeal and death

Air date:
30/09/2021
Title coined by:
Alex Horne

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the most awesome square, scale a mountain, work out the contents of some doggy bags, paint a space hopper green, and put on a medallion.

Title context

'Oatmeal and death' was what Desiree Burch thought one of the doggy-bags contained, in the 'Work out the contents of the bags' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Havregrød og død', which translates as 'Oatmeal and death'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Kaurapuuroa ja kuolemaa', which translates as 'Oatmeal and death'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Sweden's SVT Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Mount Evetest', which translates as 'Mount Evertask'. Just a pun on 'Mount Everest', in reference to the 'Scale the mountain' task, incorporating the word 'test', which is used when referring to the tasks. [Translator credit: DANI]
  • On Norway's Discovery+ Norge streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Havregrøt og død', which translates as 'Oatmeal and death'. Note: this Norwegian translation of the title only appears in the subtitles for the episode, whereas the episode title listed on the platform is the original English title. [Translator credit: Jan Harald]
