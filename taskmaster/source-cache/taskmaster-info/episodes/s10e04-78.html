Episode 4: Toshwash

Air date:
05/11/2020

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the cheekiest food, make a phone ring, persuade a security guard to look in their shopping bag, drop a water balloon as far as possible without it bursting, and roll an edible sphere across a line as slowly as possible.

Title context

'Toshwash' was a word used by Richard the security guard to describe Richard Herring's argument in the 'Persuade the security guard to look in your bag' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Det rene pladder', which translates as 'It's pure jabber'. In Danish, to say that someone 'pladdrar' means that they're talking nonsense.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Hyllynpylly'. 'Hyllynpylly' is apparently a nonsense word; the closest word to it, 'hyllypylly', basically means 'lardass'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Sweden's SVT Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Utmaning utan mottagning', which translates as 'Challenge without reception'. This title is catchier in Swedish, because it rhymes. It's unclear what it might be a reference to - perhaps the 'Make the phone ring' task? [Translator credit: DANI]
