Episode 8: Moments of silence

Air date:
03/12/2020
Title coined by:
Greg Davies

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the best thing with wings, fill a cup to overflowing without touching a red green, learn their lines for a play, extract water from a coconut, and stare each other out while maintaining specific facial expressions.

Title context

'A couple of moments of silence' were called for by Greg Davies after all of the contestants disappointed him in the 'Fill the cup to overflowing without touching the red green' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Stille øjeblikke', which translates as 'Silent moments'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Hiljaisia hetkiä', which translates as 'Silent moments'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Sweden's SVT Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Mordgåta med manusminnesförlust', which translates as 'Murder mystery with script amnesia'. This is a reference to the 'Learn your lines and perform a scene' task. [Translator credit: DANI]
  • On Norway's Discovery+ Norge streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Et øyeblikks stillhet', which translates as 'A moment of silence'. Note: this Norwegian translation of the title only appears in the subtitles for the episode, whereas the episode title listed on the platform is the original English title. [Translator credit: Jan Harald]
