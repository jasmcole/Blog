Episode 3: Dafty in the middle

Air date:
13/10/2022
Title coined by:
Fern Brady

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the thing they most want on a Sunday morning, put the most sand into a shopping trolley, identify some unusual ice cream flavours, spectacularly catch something, and then predict whether a series of sounds will be louder or quieter than each other.

Title context

'Dafty in the middle' was how Fern Brady referred to John Kearns, during the 'Put the most sand in the shopping trolley' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Tumpen i midten', which translates as 'Dunce in the middle'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Älykääpio remmissä', which translates as 'Half-wit in the group'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Sweden's SVT Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Händerna på höften', which translates as 'Hands on hips'. This is a reference to the 'Put the most sand in the shopping trolley / Sabotage your team' task.
  • On Norway's Discovery+ Norge streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Den treige i midten', which translates as 'The slow one in the middle'. Note: this Norwegian translation of the title only appears in the subtitles for the episode, whereas the episode title listed on the platform is the original English title. [Translator credit: Jan Harald]
