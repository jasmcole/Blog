Episode 8: The umbrella wink

Air date:
16/05/2024
Title coined by:
Greg Davies

Episode synopsis

In this episode, the contestants must: bring in the thing that Greg would most like to squeeze in his mighty hands; flick, flip, and flap a flip-flop, flan, and flapjack; put a watermelon on a table while completing secret tasks; bring a mannequin to life; and write answers to Greg’s questions based on a letter they have chosen.

Title context

Greg tells Nick Mohammed that “the umbrella wink” is going to catch on, after the ‘Put the watermelon on the table while completing secret tasks’ task, in which Nick winks directly at the camera almost every time that he says the word ‘umbrella’.

Alternate Titles

  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Sateenvarjosilmänisku', which translates as 'Umbrella wink'. [Translator credit: yellowrainboot]
