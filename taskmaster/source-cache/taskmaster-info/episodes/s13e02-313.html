Episode 2: Birdy hand finger

Air date:
21/04/2022
Title coined by:
Bridget Christie

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the object that they'd most like to show to an alien, either tie a ribbon around or throw a ping pong ball at a maypole, recreate the coolest photo on their phone, score an exercise ball hat-trick, and draw the strange objects under their tables.

Title context

'Birdy hand finger' was what Bridget Christie decided she had drawn, during the 'Draw and describe the object under your table' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Fugleagtig finger', which translates as 'Bird-like finger'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Lintukäsisormi', which translates as 'Birdy hand finger'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Sweden's SVT Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Ping pong-majstång', which translates as 'Ping Pong Maypole'. This is a reference to the 'Tie a ribbon round the maypole or hit it with a ping pong ball' task. [Translator credit: DANI]

    Hello, and welcome to another edition of Taskmaster. I'm Greg Davies. I'm middle-aged, but the pheromones I emit still compel sailor-filled ships to dash themselves onto the rocks. Watch them work their magic as five sensible, and in some cases, intelligent comedians get angry about rubber ducks. All for the chance to take home a trophy that is yet to make its £3 reserve on eBay. Feast your eyes on their hopeful little faces.
