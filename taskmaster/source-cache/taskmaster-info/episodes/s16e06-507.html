Episode 6: Brother Alex

Air date:
26/10/2023
Title coined by:
Lucy Beaumont

Episode synopsis

In this episode, the contestants must bring in the greatest thing that makes quite a loud noise when shaken, avoid two rubbish robots while blindfolded, recreate the garage scene in the lab, present a powerful piece called ‘Heads, Shoulders, Knees and Toes’, and play a game of pineapple bluff.

Title context

“Brother Alex” is what Lucy Beaumont calls the character played by Alex Horne in her powerful production titled ‘Heads, Shoulders, Knees and Toes’.

Alternate Titles

  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Veli Alex', which translates as 'Brother Alex'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Sweden's SVT Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Broder Alex', which translates as 'Brother Alex'.
  • On Norway's Discovery+ Norge streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Bror Alex', which translates as 'Brother Alex'. Note: this Norwegian translation of the title only appears in the subtitles for the episode, whereas the episode title listed on the platform is the original English title. [Translator credit: Jan Harald]
