Episode 7: Heg

Air date:
26/05/2022
Title coined by:
Judi Love

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants bring in the nicest thing to put your finger in, become show-offs, deliver a cocktail to Greg, create and then eat an edible sculpture, and then attempt to build the tallest toilet roll tower.

Title context

'Heg' was what Judi Love claimed she had heard, after she apparently did not know who Greg Davies was, during the 'Deliver a cocktail to Greg' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Heg'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Kuka on Greg?', which translates as 'Who is Greg?'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Sweden's SVT Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Skakad - inte rörd', which translates as 'Shaken - not stirred'. This is a reference to the 'Deliver a cocktail to Greg' task. [Source credit: DANI]
