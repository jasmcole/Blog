Episode 2: Look at me

Air date:
02/05/2017
Title coined by:
Greg Davies

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the most boastful item, keep a basketball on a treadmill, paint a portrait of the Taskmaster without touching a mat, get as much flour onto a target as possible, put an egg in an eggcup, and make the longest balloon chain.

Title context

'Look at me!' was something Greg Davies barked at the contestants during the 'Make the longest balloon chain' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Se på mig', which translates as 'Look at me'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Katsokaa minua', which translates as 'Look at me'. [Translator credit: zador]
