Episode 8: You tuper super

Air date:
02/06/2022
Title coined by:
Judi Love

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the loveliest thing to hold above your head, hold a five minute conversation in Swedish, keep their tongues poked out for as long as possible, walk through a door wearing the longest shoes and biggest hat, and then write down things that fit in certain categories.

Title context

'You tuper super' was something Judi Love said to Fred the Swede, during the 'Learn Swedish' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Duper også super', which translates as 'Duper also super'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Pakkoruotsia', which translates as 'Obligatory Swedish'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Sweden's SVT Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Prata med en svensk', which translates as 'Talk to a Swede'. This is a reference to the 'Have a conversation in Swedish' task. [Source credit: DANI]
