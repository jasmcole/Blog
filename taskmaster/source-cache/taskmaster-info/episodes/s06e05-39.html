Episode 5: H

Air date:
30/05/2018
Title coined by:
Asim Chaudhry

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the sturdiest thing, work out the length of a piece of string, remove a tablecloth from underneath some eggs, identify a mystery link, pull off an elaborate snooker trick shot, and throw eggs onto a shelf.

Title context

'H' was a letter uttered an incredible number of times by Asim Chaudhry as he failed to help his team solve the 'Identify the link and do it 100 times' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'H!'.
