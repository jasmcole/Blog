Episode 10: He was a different man

Air date:
04/07/2018
Title coined by:
Liza Tarbuck

Episode synopsis

In this series finale, the contestants must bring in the least appropriate wedding accessory, knock over the fewest bowling pins, blow out a candle from a distance, declare their love for the Taskmaster, and then pick numbers higher and lower than the people next to them.

Title context

'He was a different man' was how Liza Tarbuck described Alex, post cake-sitting, in the 'Declare your love for the Taskmaster' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Han blev en helt anden mand', which translates as 'He was a completely different man'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Hän oli kuin toinen mies', which translates as 'He was like another man'. [Translator credit: zador]
  • On Norway's Discovery+ Norge streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Han var en annen mann', which translates as 'He was a different man'. Note: this Norwegian translation of the title only appears in the subtitles for the episode, whereas the episode title listed on the platform is the original English title. [Translator credit: Jan Harald]
