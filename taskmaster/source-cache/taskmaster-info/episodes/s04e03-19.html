Episode 3: Hollowing out a baguette

Air date:
09/05/2017
Title coined by:
Hugh Dennis

Episode synopsis

In this episode the contestants must bring in the best subscription, camouflage themselves, make a Taskmaster movie trailer, persuade three animals to stand on a mat, transfer water from one fishbowl to another, and play a game of musical words. In the thrilling tie-breaker task, two contestants must decant wine from one bottle to another from atop an umpire's chair.

Title context

'Hollowing out a baguette' was something that Hugh Dennis narrated himself as doing, during the 'Transfer water from one fishbowl to another' task.

Alternate Titles

  • On Denmark's TV2 Play streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Udhulning af en baguette', which translates as 'Hollowing out a baguette'.
  • On Finland's MTV Katsomo streaming platform, this episode is titled 'Hän kovertaa patonkia', which translates as 'He hollows out a baguette'. [Translator credit: zador]
