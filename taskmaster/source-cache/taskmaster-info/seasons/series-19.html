Season synopsis

Series 19 of Taskmaster UK began airing on the 1st of May 2025, featuring Fatiha El-Ghorri, Jason Mantzoukas, Mathew Baynton, Rosie Ramsey, and Stevie Martin.

The off-site task filming location for the series is Painshill park in Surrey.

The studio filming sessions took place at Pinewood Studios between the 18th and 24th of September 2024.

The first episode of the series was premiered at a live event in New York on the 21st of January 2025.

Notes

  • On the 15th of March 2023, it was reported that Channel 4 had commissioned an additional six seasons of Taskmaster UK.
  • On 19th August 2024, applications opened up via SRO Audiences for tickets to attend the studio filming sessions at Pinewood Studios between the 18th and 24th of September 2024.
  • The cast was officially announced on the 22nd of January 2025, the day after the first episode was premiered at a live event in New York.
  • On the 10th of April 2025, British Comedy Guide listed the season as airing from the 1st of May 2025.
