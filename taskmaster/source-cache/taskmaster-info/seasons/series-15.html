Season synopsis

Series 15, featuring contestants Frankie Boyle, Ivo Graham, Jenny Eclair, Kiell Smith-Bynoe, and Mae Martin, began airing on the 30th of March 2023.

The live studio recordings took place at Pinewood Studios in Iver Heath, near London, between the 22nd and the 28th of September 2022.

Notes

  • Tickets for the live studio filming sessions of this series were made available via SRO Audiences' website on the 26th of August 2022, with sessions scheduled for the 22nd, 23rd, 26th, 27th, and 28th of September 2022 at Pinewood Studios.
  • On the 1st of December 2022, the contestants for the series were revealed as Frankie Boyle, Ivo Graham, Jenny Eclair, Kiell Smith-Bynoe, and Mae Martin.
  • On the 15th of March 2023, early press images for the season were published on Channel 4's website, indicating that the season would begin airing in 'week 13', i.e. on the 30th of March 2023.
  • On the 16th of March 2023, it was confirmed that the show would begin airing on the 30th of March 2023.
