Season synopsis

The sixth series of Taskmaster, consisting of ten episodes for the first time, was originally broadcast on Dave between May and July 2018.

It featured contestants  Alice Levine, Asim Chaudhry, Liza Tarbuck, Russell Howard, and Tim Vine.

The live studio sessions were recorded at Pinewood Studios in Iver Heath, near London.
