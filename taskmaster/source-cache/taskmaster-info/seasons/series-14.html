Season synopsis

Series 14, featuring the contestants Dara Ó Briain, Fern Brady, John Kearns, Munya Chawawa, and Sarah Millican, began airing on the 29th of September 2022.

The live studio recordings for the series took place between the 28th of April and the 5th of May 2022 at Pinewood Studios in Iver Heath, near London.

Notes

  • The cast for the series was revealed on the 23rd of June 2022, immediately after the airing of the second Champion of Champions special.
  • On the 14th of September 2022, the first low-res promotional images were released on Channel 4's press site for 'week 39', indicating that the show would likely start airing on the 29th of September 2022, and also revealing that the contestant teams for the series would be Dara, Fern, and John, versus Munya and Sarah. The images also revealed that some location tasks were filmed at Gatwick airport's South Terminal, the re-opening of which Alex had appeared in a promotional video for, back in March 2022.
  • Later the same day, the air date of the first episode was officially confirmed as being on the 29th of September 2022.
