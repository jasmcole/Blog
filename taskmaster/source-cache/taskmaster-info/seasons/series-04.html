Season synopsis

The fourth series of Taskmaster, consisting of eight episodes, was originally broadcast on Dave between April and June 2017, and featured contestants Hugh Dennis, Joe Lycett, Lolly Adefope, Mel Giedroyc, and Noel Fielding.

This was the first series of the show where the live studio sessions were recorded at Pinewood Studios in Iver Heath, near London.

Notes

  • Based on the account of someone who attended the filming of episode 5, it appears that this series was filmed between the 6th and the 9th of March 2017.
