Season synopsis

The seventh series of Taskmaster, consisting of ten episodes, was originally broadcast on Dave between September and November 2018.

It featured contestants James Acaster, Jessica Knappett, Kerry Godliman, Phil Wang, and Rhod Gilbert.

The live studio sessions were recorded at Pinewood Studios in Iver Heath, near London.
