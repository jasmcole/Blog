Season synopsis

The eleventh series of Taskmaster, consisting of ten episodes, was originally broadcast on Channel 4 between March and May 2021.

The contestants for the series were Charlotte Ritchie, Jamali Maddix, Lee Mack, Mike Wozniak, and Sarah Kendall.

It was the second full series of the show to be filmed during the Covid-19 pandemic, with social distancing measures having to be implemented both in the pre-recorded tasks, and in the studio recording sessions, which took place at Pinewood Studios in Iver Heath, near London.

The recordings were later shown to socially-distanced audiences at The Curzon Cinema, Soho, in October 2020, with the audience reactions being taped and added to the episodes before broadcast.

Notes on discrepancies

  • The final series scores are affected by a discrepancy in the first episode of the series, where Mike Wozniak officially received one more point than he was observed to have been awarded, and Sarah Kendall officially received one less point than she was observed to have been awarded.
  • Fortunately, this discrepancy did not affect any of the contestants' final standings in that episode, or in the series as a whole.
