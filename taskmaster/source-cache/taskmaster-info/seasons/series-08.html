Season synopsis

The eighth series of Taskmaster, consisting of ten episodes, was originally broadcast on Dave between May and July 2019.

It featured contestants Iain Stirling, Joe Thomas, Lou Sanders, Paul Sinha, and Sian Gibson.

The live studio sessions were recorded at Pinewood Studios in Iver Heath, near London.
