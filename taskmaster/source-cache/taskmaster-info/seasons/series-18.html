Season synopsis

Series 18 of Taskmaster UK started airing on the 12th of September 2024.

The cast of the series is Andy Zaltzman, Babatunde Aléshé, Emma Sidi, Jack Dee, and Rosie Jones.

The studio recording sessions took place between the 13th and 21st of May 2024.

Notes

  • On the 15th of March 2023, it was reported that Channel 4 had commissioned an additional six seasons of Taskmaster UK.
  • On the 3rd of April 2024, applications opened up via SRO Audiences for tickets to attend the studio filming sessions between the 13th and 21st of May 2024.
  • On the 30th of May, the cast was revealed as Andy Zaltzman, Babatunde Aléshé, Emma Sidi, Jack Dee, and Rosie Jones.
  • According to Digiguide.TV and British Comedy Guide, the series is scheduled to begin airing on the 12th of September 2024.
  • On the 28th of August 2024, it was confirmed on the official Taskmaster social media accounts that the series would begin airing from the 12th of September.
