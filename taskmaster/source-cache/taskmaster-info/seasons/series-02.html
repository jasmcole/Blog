Season synopsis

The second series of Taskmaster, consisting of just five episodes, originally aired on Dave between June and July 2016, and featured contestants Doc Brown, Joe Wilkinson, Jon Richardson, Katherine Ryan, and Richard Osman.

The series was filmed back-to-back with series 3, with the live studio sessions taking place at the now closed Fountain Studios in London.
