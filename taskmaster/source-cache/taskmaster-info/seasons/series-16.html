Season synopsis

Series 16 of Taskmaster UK began airing on 21 September 2023.

The cast for the series are Julian Clary, Lucy Beaumont, Sam Campbell, Sue Perkins, and Susan Wokoma.

Filming of the live studio segments took place at Pinewood Studios between the 2nd and 12th of May 2023.

Notes

  • On the 15th of March 2023, it was reported that Channel 4 had commissioned an additional six seasons of Taskmaster UK.
  • On the 27th of March 2023, applications opened for tickets for the live studio filming sessions at Pinewood Studios, which were scheduled to take place between the 2nd and 12th of May 2023.
  • On the 1st of June 2023, the cast for the season was officially revealed as Julian Clary, Lucy Beaumont, Sam Campbell, Sue Perkins, and Susan Wokoma.
  • On the 5th of September 2023, it was disclosed on the British Comedy Guide website that the season would begin airing on the 21st of September 2023.
  • The air date for the season was confirmed by Channel 4 on the 6th of September 2023.
