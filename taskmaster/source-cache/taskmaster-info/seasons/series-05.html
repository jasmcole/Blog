Season synopsis

The fifth series of Taskmaster, consisting of eight episodes, was originally broadcast on Dave between September and November 2017, and featured contestants Aisling Bea, Bob Mortimer, Mark Watson, Nish Kumar, and Sally Phillips.

The live studio sessions were recorded between the 3rd and 6th of July 2017 at Pinewood Studios in Iver Heath, near London.

Notes

  • The cast for the season, and the live studio recording dates, were revealed on the 13th of June 2017.
