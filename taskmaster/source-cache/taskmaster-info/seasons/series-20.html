Season synopsis

Series 20 of Taskmaster UK, featuring Ania Magliano, Maisie Adam, Phil Ellis, Reece Shearsmith, and Sanjeev Bhaskar, began airing on the 11th of September 2025.

The studio recording sessions took place at Pinewood Studios between the 7th and 17th of May 2025.

Notes

  • On the 15th of March 2023, it was reported that Channel 4 had commissioned an additional six seasons of Taskmaster UK.
  • On the 15th of April 2025, applications opened up for tickets to attend the studio recording sessions at Pinewood Studios between the 7th and 17th of May 2025.
  • On the 3rd of July 2025, the cast was announced as Ania Magliano, Maisie Adam, Phil Ellis, Reece Shearsmith, and Sanjeev Bhaskar.
  • On the 27th of August 2025, it was confirmed that the series would begin airing on the 11th of September 2025.
